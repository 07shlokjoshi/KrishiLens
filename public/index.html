Unreadable Label

CORRECTIVE FIELD ACTIONS:
  • Ensure the camera lens is clean and free of water or soil.
  • Hold the container steady at approximately 20–30 cm distance.
  • Rotate the bottle to align the statutory CIB&RC toxicity diamond.
  • In dim conditions, turn on the flashlight or use photo upload.
