---

## Key Points

1. **Validation is mandatory** before every training
2. **Source tracking** via MERGE_MANIFEST.json
3. **Duplicate detection** prevents data leakage
4. **Negative controls** included in all splits
5. **YOLO format** used throughout
6. **No automatic deployment** - always compare metrics first
7. **Frontend unchanged** - web app continues to work
